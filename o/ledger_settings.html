POS go-live date (cutover)

Before this date, sales come from Stripe and bank deposits (DaySmart era). From this date, Paragon Ink POS tickets post sales, sales tax, tips, gift cards and deposits, and bank deposits only clear Undeposited Funds / Stripe Clearing. Leave blank until you stop ringing sales in DaySmart.

Keys are never typed into this app or saved in its database. To test with bank feed's sandbox later, the owner adds them as Windows environment variables on the shop PC (System → Environment Variables): PLAID_CLIENT_ID, PLAID_SECRET, PLAID_ENV=sandbox. Going live with your bank needs a paid bank feed plan and the owner's approval. CSV/OFX import always works as the fallback.

SimpleFIN Bridge (read-only bank feed)

SimpleFIN can only read balances and transactions. It can't move money. The bank login stays with SimpleFIN; this app never sees it. Off until you paste a token.
